[Uptimy](https://www.upti.my) is a hosted service, so its code isn't here, but its bugs and feature requests are tracked here in the open. Search the [existing issues](https://github.com/uptimy/feedback/issues) first: if yours is there, add a 👍 or what's different in your case.

## Report a bug

[Open a bug report](https://github.com/uptimy/feedback/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) with what you did, what you expected and what happened instead. The page's URL and a screenshot help most.

## Request a feature

[Open a feature request](https://github.com/uptimy/feedback/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml). Describe what you're trying to do rather than the solution: there's often more than one way to get there.

## Help & support

- **Questions and setup help:** ask in [Discord](https://www.upti.my/discord), in the **#help** forum.
- **Security issues:** report them privately, see [Security](#security).
- **Uptimy account or billing:** [support@upti.my](mailto:support@upti.my).
- **Is a service down?** [@downtimy](https://x.com/downtimy) on X posts when GitHub, AWS, Cloudflare and the other services on our [status hub](https://www.upti.my/status) go down and recover.

Our open-source projects have their own repositories: [Uptimy Agent](https://github.com/uptimy/agent) (self-hosted monitoring), [uptimyctl](https://github.com/uptimy/uptimyctl) (the CLI) and the [MCP server](https://github.com/uptimy/mcp) (for AI assistants). Report their bugs there.

## Security

Please **don't open a public issue**. Report it privately: on this repository's [Security tab](https://github.com/uptimy/feedback/security), choose **Report a vulnerability**. You'll hear back within 3 working days.

## Code of conduct

Be kind and constructive. We follow the [Contributor Covenant](https://github.com/uptimy/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), here and in Discord.
